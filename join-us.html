Who we are

BYTE Play has had it's humble beginnings under several different names. You may have seen us go by Nova Networks. We're not a large company. We're a tight group of developers, builders, and community managers who genuinely care about every feature we ship.

We run Fertilia, a farming progression RPG that receives weekly updates. Every enchant, boss fight, and seasonal event is designed and built by us. Nothing is outsourced. Nothing is generic.

"We'd rather ship one polished thing than ten mediocre ones."

We're looking to expand the development team.
